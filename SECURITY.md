## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us as follows:

1. **Email**: Send an email to [titikshabansal0209@gmail.com](mailto:titikshabansal0209@gmail.com) with the subject line "Security Vulnerability Report". Please include the following details in your report:
   - A description of the vulnerability.
   - Steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
   - Potential impact of the vulnerability.
   - Any possible solutions or mitigations.

2. **Issue Tracker**: For less sensitive vulnerabilities, you can open an issue in our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/TitikshaBansal/ImageEditor/issues) tracker. Please label the issue with "Security".

## Response Process

1. **Acknowledgement**: We will acknowledge the receipt of your vulnerability report within 48 hours and provide you with an estimated time frame for a more detailed response.
   
2. **Investigation**: Our security team will investigate the reported vulnerability and determine the impact and severity. During this phase, we may reach out to you for additional information or clarification.
   
3. **Resolution**: Once the investigation is complete, we will take appropriate measures to resolve the vulnerability, which may include:
   - Patching the vulnerability.
   - Providing mitigation steps.
   - Releasing a new version of the software.
   
4. **Notification**: After the vulnerability has been resolved, we will notify you and thank you for your contribution. We may also include your name in the project’s contributors list, with your permission.

## Public Disclosure

We will publicly disclose the vulnerability after a resolution has been implemented and the new version has been released. We believe in responsible disclosure and prefer to collaborate with security researchers to keep our users safe.

## Security Best Practices

To help keep your installation secure, we recommend the following best practices:

- Always use the latest version of ImageEditor.
- Regularly check for updates and security patches.
- Report any suspicious activity or potential vulnerabilities immediately.
- Follow the principle of least privilege when granting access to your systems and data.
